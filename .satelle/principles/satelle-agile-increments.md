# Agile increments — one story, one commit

Split a batch of feedback or a broad goal into separate stories, one observable
change each, and drive them one at a time ([[satelle-start-small]],
[[satelle-agent-goals]]). Each story lands as **one focused commit** whose
message names what changed and ends with the story id: never fold several
stories into one commit, nor one story across unrelated commits.
