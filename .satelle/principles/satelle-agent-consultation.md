# Agent consultation

1. **Talking decides nothing.** A rework relay or consultant yields context:
   a transcript, a better re-presentation. It moves no status.
2. **Every participant works from the story and its ACs**; a transcript is
   never a criterion.
3. **Consultation is not review.** A consultant's READY is a signal to the
   orchestrator; the reviewer on the edge still runs cold and one-shot, and
   its verdict alone advances the stage.
4. **Bounded, then blocked.** Re-present a rejected edge only while the round
   carries new evidence or a narrower fix. Park to `blocked` as soon as a
   round would bring nothing new, or when the relay or reject budget is
   spent, quoting the last objection and the decision needed. Never lower an
   AC, skip the gate, or spend past the declared maximum.
