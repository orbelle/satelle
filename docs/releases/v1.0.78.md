## [1.0.78] - 2026-11-15

### Added
- satelled colours the conventional tags priority (by level), surface, reporter and source, and filters by `priority:<level>`; `[tag_colours]` in `.satelle/ui.toml` overrides a colour or colours another tag (sto_856572ef)
- `satelle help` lists the conventional tags, and `satelle validate` warns on a priority value other than high, medium or low (sto_856572ef)

### Changed
- satelled shows a story's workflow as a coloured chip beside its category; the Stories list has no Workflow column and its space goes to Title and Progress (sto_856572ef)
